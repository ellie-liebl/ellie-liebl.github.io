Language documentation involving diaspora communities presents a combination of challenges and opportunities for approaches leveraging large data collection and assisted transcription and annotation. Demonstrating our projects on Kichwa and Mapudungun, we present a suite of our computational tools designed to effectively work with diaspora communities for language documentation.
