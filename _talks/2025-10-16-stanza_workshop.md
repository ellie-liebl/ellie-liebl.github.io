"Stanza is a collection of accurate and efficient tools for the linguistic analysis of many human languages." [https://stanfordnlp.github.io/stanza/] This workshop presents a beginner's tutorial to using Stanford NLP's Stanza tools to automatically annotate text corpora. Find the associated Google Colab notebook here: [https://colab.research.google.com/drive/1s8Wogksq58fkncxHjKG87dfc-BvxVfnp?usp=sharing]
