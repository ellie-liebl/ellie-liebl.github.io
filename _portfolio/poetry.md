## Fermi Calculations

The distance between \
your lips and mine \
is negligible \
let’s round down \
to zero 

## Ghosts

Cotton rolls into the skies above us \
A gentle thumb brushes hair from my brow \
Rough winds chafe my cheeks as I wait and rust \
Deftly folded paper becomes a crow \
 \
The glittering moon on a rippled glass \
Fingers numb, curled there, into your back \
Falling into frostbite, against the grass \
I feel only your head as all fades black \
 \
My thoughts boil away into the river \
I see you there, dancing in my mind's eye \
Running into the thicket, bamboo briar \
Then you pull my heart to your chest and sigh \
\
I'm haunted, naturally, ever since \
Seeing pictures no words would dare to mince 

## Moving Forward

Happiness isn't what it seems \
Abandoning those childhood dreams \
Flower buds freeze and the bees leave \
Seasons change and so must we 

## Miscellaneous Haikus

Warm sunlit puddles \
Sprawling on yellow blankets \
January sun \
 \
Frayed yellow blankets \
Passive search for happiness \
Crawling into bed \
 \
Silence next to you \
Awkward stranger, stagnating \
Trying to move on \
 \
Your grey suede sneakers \
Draped, casually, off your chair \
Preparing to run \
 \
Blooming out of red \
Fading towards a centric pink \
Dying carnations \
 \
Clicky clicky pen \
Stuttering down my blank page \
Poetry follows \
 \
And now, here I sit \
Listening, as I often \ 
Find myself doing \
 \
Listening to you \
As you describe to me your day \
All the things you saw
